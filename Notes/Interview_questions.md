# If i know your IP Address , how do i find your MAC address ?

    if you know my ip address then you find my MAC address by looking at
    ARP cache first and if you don't find my MAC Address there then you
    will send ARP request to all the devices on the network ... saying
    who has this ip address ... then i will reply with arp saying that
    That's my IP address and here is my MAC address ...so that is how you 
    can find my MAC address ...
