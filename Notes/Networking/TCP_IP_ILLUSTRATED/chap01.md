# Introduction
  * Effective Communication depends on a use of common language which is true for both humans and machines as well .
  Because one needs to understand what other is saying or trying to mean . Because computers don't have the intuition
  that humans have .. we built the protocols for computers so they can talk with each other regardless of what type
  of machine they are and who manufactured them etc ...
  
  ## Architectural Principles 
  * Tcp/IP protocol suite allows computers , smartphones , embedded machines of all sizes , supplied from many different
  computer vendors and running totally different softwares to communicate with each other .
  for Example : a windows computer can talk with linux , openbsd , mac if They all understand the Tcp/ip and how to parse
  them and make sense of it .And in a sense That is all the protocol is all about .

  ## PACKETS 
  * Packets , Chunks of bytes transmitted over a network independently and Packets from different sources can be mixed 
  together and pull apart later which is called multiplexing . it is possible because every packets contains the metadata
  of its ip and destination ip and some other information etc .
